Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Toowoomba Kitchen Renovations (toowoombakitchenrenovations.com.au) is a referral and quote service. We don't renovate kitchens ourselves. We pass your enquiry to one local kitchen renovator so they can quote for your project. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • The details you enter in the quote form: name, phone number, email address, suburb, kitchen work, budget range, timeframe, property type and your message.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited and device type, which may be collected through Google Analytics using cookies once analytics is enabled on this site.

How we use it

We use your details only to arrange your quote. We share them with one local kitchen renovator who services your area, so they can contact you about the project and a price. That business is responsible for how it uses your details from then on. We also keep a record of enquiries so we can report how many enquiries the site generates.

We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to marketing lists.

Storage and security

Form submissions are processed by our form provider, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), and delivered by email. We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep enquiries for up to 12 months, after which they are deleted.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes data as described in Google's privacy policy. You can block cookies in your browser without affecting your ability to request a quote.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing quotes@toowoombakitchenrenovations.com.au. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
